Calculation notes
  • Row counts vs. unique Lead IDs follow the original sheet formulas: Total Leads, Connect Rate numerator, Total Leads Considered and Total Connected Successfully count unique Lead IDs; everything else counts rows.
  • Most tables apply Cluster + Contact Timestamp filters only. Creation Date is applied only where the original formula used it (Connect Rate, Call Outcome list, Total Leads Considered; Total Connected Successfully uses the lower bound only).
  • Matching of Yes/No and text values is case-insensitive and ignores surrounding spaces.
  • Primary Blockers use substring matching (like SEARCH()); a cell with several blockers counts once under each blocker, so the Total row is a sum of blocker mentions.
  • Aggregated figures only — no customer-level data is displayed.
